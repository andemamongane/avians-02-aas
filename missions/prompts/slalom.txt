Race through the slalom: get from the pad into the finish box, 120 m north, and
stay inside it for 2 seconds, within 45 seconds of the start. Three walls stand
in the way, with gaps alternating east, west, east; never touch a wall. Stay at
or below 20 metres. Then fly back through the course the same way and land on
the pad within a minute of leaving the finish. Fly it fast.
